Our Crops

Wheat

Wheat is the foundation of India's food security and is most cultivated in Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Punjab. By connecting with AgriTech India, farmers use high-yield varieties like HD-2967 and DBW-187 which give 45-50 quintals per acre. Drone-based monitoring and precision sowing reduce labour costs by 30% and seed usage by 20%. A timely spray schedule is maintained to protect against spot blight and rust so that quality wheat reaches the mills.

Rice

Rice is the identity of Indian cuisine and is grown most in West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, and Andhra Pradesh. AgriTech India encourages farmers to use the SRI method and direct seeding, saving 40% water and increasing yield by 15%. Hybrid varieties like Sahbhagi Dhan and Stress Tolerant Dhan are used which give good yield even with less water. Our target is to ensure safe and tasty rice reaches the market without spoiling.

Cotton

Cotton is vital to the Indian textile industry, with Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Telangana being its major producing states. BT cotton seeds reduce pest infestation and micro-irrigation increases yield by up to 25%. AgriTech India controls pink bollworm and whitefly through integrated pest management. Farmers are taught timely picking and grading so that ginning mills receive premium quality cotton and farmers get better prices.

Sugarcane

Sugarcane is grown most in Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka, and is the lifeline of the sugar industry. AgriTech India's drip fertigation system increases both cane weight and sugar content. Using high-sugar varieties like Co-0238, farmers can achieve 35% higher sugar recovery. Techniques like ratoon management, earthing up, and trash mulching reduce farming costs and double farmers' income every year.

Sunflower

Sunflower seed oil is considered very healthy, with Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Andhra Pradesh being its major growing regions. AgriTech India provides farmers with hybrid varieties like KBSH-44 and DRSF-108 which yield 12-15 quintals per acre. Proper spacing, timely weeding, and aphid control improve oil quality. Sunflower cultivation also improves soil health as its roots go deep and keep the soil fertile.

Potato

Potato is the most widely grown vegetable in India, especially in Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, and Bihar. AgriTech India provides farmers with disease-resistant varieties like Kufri Jyoti and Kufri Sindhuri which protect against late blight. Seed treatment, timely earthing up, and hilling lead to better tuber formation. With cold storage and grading solutions, farmers can sell potatoes at the right time and earn 40% more profit.

Wheat Harvest Video

Modern machinery has completely transformed wheat harvesting. With combine harvesters, cutting, threshing, and cleaning happen simultaneously, saving 80% of the time. AgriTech India provides harvester renting services and operator training so even small landowners can benefit from this technology. Harvesting at the right time reduces grain damage and maintains a germination percentage of up to 95%, getting farmers better market prices.

Rice Paddies

The view of lush green paddy fields is nature's priceless gift, made possible only with proper water management. The System of Rice Intensification saves 50% water through wider plant spacing and also shortens crop duration. Using aerobic rice in puddle paddy reduces greenhouse gas emissions by up to 30%. AgriTech India teaches all these methods to farmers for better yield while protecting nature through modern techniques.

Water Management

Water management is most important in cotton farming because this crop demands the most water in summer. Micro irrigation delivers water directly to the plant roots, preventing surface evaporation. AgriTech India informs farmers about tensiometers and soil moisture sensors which indicate when and how much water to give. This saves water while also improving crop health and making cotton bolls easier to pick.

Drone Monitoring

Monitoring vast sugarcane fields has now become easy with drones, which can survey 50 acres in an hour. Multispectral cameras clearly reveal nitrogen deficiency, water stress, and disease-affected areas. AgriTech India provides variable rate application technology where fertilizers and medicines are applied only where needed. This reduces costs by 20% and decreases environmental pollution for the benefit of society.

Tractor Spraying

Uniform spraying in sunflower fields is done using tractor-mounted boom sprayers which cover 8-10 feet at once. AgriTech India trains farmers in exact chemical dosage and spray timing so that expensive medicine is not wasted. With correct pressure and nozzle size, medicine reaches even the smallest plants, protecting against white rust and alternaria leaf spot. Timely spraying reduces crop loss by up to 60%.

Greenhouse Potato

Off-season potato farming is possible through greenhouses and polytunnels, allowing farmers to get better prices in the off-season. In net houses, controlling temperature and humidity reduces aphid and jassid infestation. AgriTech India teaches farmers about tissue culture seeds, cocopeat-based nurseries, and drip fertigation for better yield. Potato yield in a polyhouse is 40% higher than open-field farming, helping increase farmer income for a prosperous enterprise.
