Modern Farming & Irrigation

Bringing revolution in farming through modern technology.

Modern Techniques

Drip Irrigation

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the plant roots, saving 40-60% water and increasing crop yield by 20-30%. Through pipes and emitters, water droplets are given to each plant individually, preventing wasteful surface runoff. AgriTech India provides complete information on drip system design, filter maintenance, and fertigation schedules so that urea and potash reach the plants directly along with water. Proper installation ensures the system works for up to 10 years without failure, recovering the farmer's investment quickly.

Sprinkler System

Sprinkler systems spray rain-like droplets, making them most suitable for wheat, sugarcane, and vegetables. Using rainguns or mini sprinklers, 2-5 acres can be watered at once, powered by borewells or motors. AgriTech India teaches farmers pressure calculation, lateral spacing, and watering cycles so that crops get neither too much nor too little water. Proper irrigation scheduling saves 35% water and improves soil health, as the soil's salinity level remains under control always.

Smart / IoT Irrigation

IoT sensors send live data on soil moisture, temperature, and conductivity to a mobile app, allowing farmers to turn motors on or off from anywhere. Soil moisture sensors are installed up to 30 cm deep, indicating when water should be given. AgriTech India provides low-cost IoT kits that are solar powered and work 24 hours. Automation ensures motors run only when needed, saving 40% electricity and completely eliminating over-irrigation through smart methods.

Drone Spraying

With agricultural drones, 10 acres of crops can be sprayed with medicine or fertilizer in just 30 minutes—work that previously required 5 people an entire day. Drones with 10-16 litre tanks perform ULV spraying with less water and more concentrated chemicals. AgriTech India provides license training for drone operators and per-acre spraying services for farmers. Uniform spraying by drones reaches every plant equally, improving crop health and reducing labour costs by up to 70% for the farmer's benefit.

Precision Farming

Precision farming performs live analysis of the field through GPS, GIS, and satellite imagery so that fertilizer, seeds, and water are used in exact calculated amounts. Variable rate technology allows different quantities of fertilizers to be applied in different parts of the field. AgriTech India provides farmers with GIS mapping, yield monitoring, and soil testing services, creating a prosperity map of the field. This reduces farming costs by 25-30% and improves yield quality by 15%, which is crucial for increasing farmers' income over time.

Greenhouse Farming

In greenhouses, temperature, humidity, and CO2 levels are controlled, allowing off-season vegetables and flowers to be grown. In polyhouses, drip fertigation and shade nets improve soil health through vermicomposting. AgriTech India provides services ranging from low-cost poly tunnels to fully automated climate-controlled greenhouses. Tomato, capsicum, cucumber, and nurseries are much more profitable in greenhouses because their demand and prices in the market are higher in the off-season.

Water Flow System

Surface irrigation has improved further with the use of siphon pipes and gated pipes for channeling water into fields. This controls water spread uniformly and delivers equal water to all parts of the field. AgriTech India advises farmers on laser land leveling which improves field evenness and saves 30% water. Proper water management prevents groundwater depletion and maintains the fertile power of the topsoil for farming.

Aerial View Irrigation

Aerial views of fields captured by drones improve irrigation system planning and monitoring. Through drones, the field's topography, slope, and water stagnation areas become clearly visible, helping identify the right places for drainage and bunding. AgriTech India tells farmers the crop health index through NDVI mapping, separating areas with water stress. Aerial surveys provide complete understanding of the field, enabling proper corrective measures for better production.

Healthy Crops

IoT sensors can live-monitor crop nitrate uptake, chlorophyll content, and water stress, helping farmers make timely nutrient management decisions. Smart devices give EC and pH readings deep into the soil, indicating which fertilizer should be given. AgriTech India provides a decision support system that merges weather forecasts with soil data to create daily advisory. Timely spraying reduces crop loss by up to 50% and doubles profits.

Machine Spraying

Tractor-mounted boom sprayers provide uniform spraying over an 8-12 meter swath, reaching both the top and bottom of the crop evenly. Air-assisted sprayers deliver medicine deep inside the plant, which was not possible with manual pumps. AgriTech India trains farmers in nozzle selection, chemical calibration, and spraying according to wind direction. Automatic boom levelers adjust height while turning, ensuring uniform coverage so that the field suffers no loss under any condition.

Modern Harvesting

With combine harvesters, cutting, threshing, and cleaning of paddy, wheat, and soybean happen in one go. Modern harvesters also have GPS yield mapping which shows which part of the field produces the highest yield. AgriTech India informs farmers about custom hiring centers where machines can be rented. Proper cylinder speed, concave clearance, and sieve adjustment ensure zero grain damage, producing dry, clean grain of better quality for the market.

Inside Greenhouse

Inside greenhouses, thermostatically controlled fans, pad cooling systems, and fogging machines provide an ideal environment for plants whether it's 45 degrees or 5 degrees outside. In hydroponic and cocopeat mediums, plants are grown without soil and nutrient solutions are delivered in exact doses by pumps. AgriTech India trains farmers in vertical farming, Dutch bucket systems, and NFT channels for higher yield in a small space. Pesticide residue is also completely absent in greenhouses, providing organic quality vegetables.
