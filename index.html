Revolution in India's Agriculture

AgriTech India is committed to bringing modern technology to every farmer in the country. We help double farmers' income by using digital agriculture, smart irrigation, and precision farming. Our team educates farmers about better seeds, fertilizers, and modern machinery so that crop yield increases. Join us today and see how technology makes farming easier and more profitable.

Our Services

Wheat Farming

Wheat farming is one of India's most important crops. With AgriTech India, farmers can increase their yield by up to 30% using high-yield varieties. Modern harvesting techniques reduce farming costs and improve quality. Using climate-resistant seeds, farmers can cultivate throughout the year.

Rice Cultivation

Rice is widely grown across India, and using the SRI method, farmers can save 50% water and gain 20% more yield. AgriTech India trains farmers in seed treatment, organic fertilizers, and proper irrigation techniques so that high-quality rice reaches every home.

Cotton Farming

In cotton farming, AgriTech India educates farmers on integrated pest management along with advanced BT cotton seeds, so pest control is achieved without harming the soil. Our mission is to help farmers earn profits and supply pure cotton to the nation's textile industry. Cold storage solutions also help maintain cotton quality for a longer time.

Sugarcane Crop

With drip fertigation, farmers can significantly increase sugarcane yield. Drip fertigation improves both the weight and sugar content of the cane. AgriTech India trains farmers in better varieties, scientific pruning, and pest control so that sugar mills receive premium quality cane and farmers' income rises.

Sunflower Farming

Sunflower seeds are used to produce edible oil, and its demand in the market is increasing daily. AgriTech India provides advanced sunflower seeds and scientific processing techniques that improve oil quality. Farmers can harvest 8 to 10 quintals per acre when cultivated correctly.

Potato Farming

Potato is the most important vegetable in Indian cuisine. With seed treatment, timely sowing, and balanced fertilizers, farmers can achieve around 250 quintals per acre. AgriTech India provides better seed treatment and cold storage solutions to increase profits and maintain potato freshness for longer.

Harvest Season

The harvest season is the most valuable time of the year when a farmer's year-long hard work bears fruit. Modern harvesting machines speed up crop cutting and reduce pest damage. AgriTech India makes combine harvesters, threshers, and modern storage solutions accessible to farmers.

Green Fields

A lush green field is nature's most beautiful display. Through the study of Vedic agriculture, farmers are taught about soil health, water management, and organic farming methods. AgriTech India fulfills the dream of green fields with scientific techniques and cow-based farming.

Water Irrigation

Smart irrigation ensures better crop health. Drip and sprinkler systems save water by delivering it directly to the plant roots. AgriTech India provides soil moisture sensors and automated irrigation controllers that work via mobile app and save 40 to 60% water.

Drone Technology

Drones have revolutionized crop monitoring and spraying. With AgriTech India, farmers can spray fertilizers and medicines 10 times faster and more evenly using drones. Aerial imaging enables accurate assessment of crop health, soil moisture, and pest presence, helping farmers make timely decisions.

Modern Tractor

GPS-based tractors are the future of farming, automatically regulating seed and fertilizer usage. AgriTech India provides farmers with precision farming technology that reduces costs and increases yield. Advanced machinery halves farming time and reduces labor expenses by up to 50%.

Greenhouse Farm

Growing off-season vegetables and flowers in a controlled environment is now easy for farmers. Greenhouse farming protects crops from intense heat, cold, or rain. AgriTech India sets up greenhouses with polytunnels and drip irrigation so farmers can grow tomatoes, capsicum, basil, and roses like flowers and vegetables throughout the year.
